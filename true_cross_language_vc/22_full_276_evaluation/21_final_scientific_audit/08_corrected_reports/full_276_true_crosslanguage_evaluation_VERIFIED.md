# FINAL SCIENTIFIC AUDIT VERSION — verified against raw result artifacts.

1. Genuine linguistic cross-language speech generation was technically successful at scale (276/276 files generated, 100.0% Audio QC pass).
2. Target-language conversion was highly reliable (Overall target LID: 99.64%).
3. Speaker identity remained substantially distinguishable from different-speaker controls (discrimination margin +0.4647).
4. A majority, but not all, pathology-related acoustic contrasts were retained (Overall: 59.1%).
5. The normalization analysis indicated that: Generated PD speech was significantly closer to the HC centroid under this feature-space distance measure, consistent with partial acoustic normalization.
6. Spanish->German produced a promising improvement in the independent 80-file validation, but this effect attenuated in the full cohort and did not persist in the development-excluded sensitivity analysis.
7. German->Spanish showed no consistent diagnostic benefit across any partition.
8. Therefore, successful language conversion and speaker preservation do not automatically translate into reliable cross-language PD-classification improvement.
